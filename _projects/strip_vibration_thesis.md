**Bachelor's Thesis, Yonsei University**

Motivated by the need for vibration control during the galvanizing process of steel sheets, I derived an analytical model for the vibration of a strip vertically supported by rollers at both ends.

**Contributions**
- Derivation of the analytical model of the strip system and its solution
- Extension of the solution to account for variable roller diameters

**Tools**: MATLAB
