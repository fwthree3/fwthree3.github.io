**M.Sc. Thesis, Yonsei University · 2010–2012**

For my master's thesis, I designed and built a walking-assistive lower limb exoskeleton for paraplegic patients, and developed a gait-phase transition algorithm that estimates the user's walking intent from center of pressure (CoP) measurements.

**Contributions**
- Full design and construction of the exoskeleton hardware, including force sensors and amplifying circuits
- Kinematic modeling and analysis of the gait phase transition
- A DSP-based sensor and communication board, with data acquisition via CAN communication

**Concurrent graduate research**: during the same period, I also worked on an exoskeleton foot module with a ground reaction force (GRF) sensor, a safety unit and circular-path tracking algorithm for a UAV (DSP-based), and a strain-gauge-based smart bed for patient fall detection with a custom amplifier board and Arduino.

**Outcomes**: 1 international journal paper, 1 domestic conference paper.
