**Part-time Research Associate, University of Waterloo**

I designed the hardware and control system for a robotic HIFU (high-intensity focused ultrasound) pet treatment device.

**Contributions**
- Design of a 3-axis motorized end-effector head
- Control board development, including the control algorithm, electrical circuit design, and PCB design, with wireless (Bluetooth) connectivity
- Operation GUI development

**Tools**: STM32, KiCAD (PCB design), Arduino (ESP32 for Bluetooth), Python
