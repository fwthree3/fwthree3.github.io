**Graduate Research Assistant, Yonsei University**

I developed the patient-fall measurement system for an "intellectual bed" project, using strain-gauge force sensors at the bed's legs to detect fall events.

**Contributions**
- Design and construction of the system hardware, including force sensors and amplifying circuits
- Data acquisition and communication via RS232 over Bluetooth
- Partial contribution to the overall intellectual-bed system

**Tools**: Solidworks, Arduino
