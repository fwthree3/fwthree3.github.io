**Hyundai Heavy Industries, Medical Robotics Institute · 2012–2017**

MORNING WALK is an end-effector type gait rehabilitation robot with a seating-type body weight support, designed for patients recovering mobility after stroke or spinal cord injury. I led the hardware design of the robot from concept through commercialization.

**Contributions**
- Full (100%) hardware design: concept and basic design, detailed design, and kinematics
- Actuator selection and sensor system design, validated through dynamics analysis and link structural analysis
- Design of a ground reaction force (GRF) measurement system and a torque compensation mechanism for the seating-type body weight support
- Korean FDA (now MFDS) medical device certification and installation in rehabilitation hospitals
- Transitioned to control software for 2 years: developed the training-report UI and improved gait trajectory and control logic based on medical staff feedback

**Outcomes**: 2 conference papers; 1 US/EP/CN/JP/KR patent family, 1 US/KR patent, and 8 Korean patents related to the robot's mechanisms.
