**Ph.D. Dissertation, University of Waterloo · 2017–2022**

My dissertation, *Safe and Efficient Navigation of a Mobile Robot: Path Planning Based on Hierarchical Topology Map and Motion Planning with Pedestrian Behavior Model*, addressed global path planning and pedestrian-aware motion planning for mobile robots operating in human environments.

**Contributions**
- A Hierarchical Topology Map with Explicit Corridor for global path planning
- Safety-aware navigation speed adjustment based on the local environment
- A pedestrian behavior model for socially-aware motion planning

**PackExpo demo**: independently developed a custom 7-DOF inverse kinematics solver and prepared an end-to-end mobile-manipulator pick-and-place demo within two months.

**Outcomes**: journal publications in *Intelligent Service Robotics* and *Control Engineering Practice*, and a conference paper at IEEE/ASME AIM 2019.
